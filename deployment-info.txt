CONTRACT OVERVIEW
-----------------
The SupplierCompliance contract manages humanitarian resource suppliers
on the Sepolia testnet. It enforces registration rules, resource
declaration, one-day compliance, automatic deactivation via a scheduled
external trigger, duration-based penalties held in escrow, and supplier
reactivation. Includes a reputation scoring system with tiered
classification.

BUSINESS RULES
--------------
- Registration fee: 100,000 wei (exact)
- Compliance period: 1 day (86,400 seconds)
- Penalty tiers: 200k / 400k / 800k / 1M wei based on days inactive
- Escrow period: 30 days of good behaviour for refund
- Reputation starts at 100, capped at 200
