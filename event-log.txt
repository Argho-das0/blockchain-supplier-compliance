EVENT LOG
=========

All events emitted by the SupplierCompliance contract during the
demonstration. Events are permanently recorded on the Hardhat blockchain
and can be observed in the DApp's Live Event Log panel or in the
Hardhat node terminal output.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT REFERENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SupplierRegistered   : emitted once per successful supplier registration
ResourceRegistered   : emitted once per resource type, per supplier
ResourceUpdated      : emitted every time a supplier updates a resource
SupplierDeactivated  : emitted when the owner deactivates a non-compliant supplier
PenaltyPaid          : emitted when a supplier pays the penalty
SupplierReactivated  : emitted when a supplier is reactivated after payment
