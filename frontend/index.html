Compliance & Penalty

Penalty Escrow

Penalty payments are held in escrow. Stay compliant for 30 days to get a full refund. Fail again within the window, and the funds go to the aid pool.

Reputation

Score starts at 100. Each resource update adds 1 (max 200). Deactivation subtracts 20 — or only 10 if the penalty is paid within 1 hour. Tiers: Platinum (180+), Gold (150+), Silver (120+), Bronze (80+), Probation (below 80).

Admin — Emergency Pause

Only the compliance officer can pause or unpause the contract. When paused, no supplier can register, register resources, or update quantities. Read-only views still work.
